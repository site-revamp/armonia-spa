# README.md — Armonia Spa Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

**Armonia Spa** is a luxury spa and wellness business in Dubai, offering professional massage treatments, traditional Moroccan baths, wellness packages and gift certificates. The business currently operates two branches: Media City and DIFC.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning Armonia as a premium wellness destination combining Western and Eastern relaxation traditions. It targets Dubai residents, professionals, hotel guests and visitors seeking quality spa experiences.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Brand Positioning
- Replaced generic spa messaging with a more distinctive luxury wellness direction.
- Highlighted Armonia's combination of massage therapies and traditional Moroccan bath experiences.
- Increased the prominence of its two Dubai locations.

**Problem addressed:** The previous headline, "Spa and Massage in Dubai", did not communicate Armonia's distinctive services or differentiate it from competing spas.

### 2. Clearer Treatment Discovery and Pricing
- Improved the organisation of massage treatments, Moroccan baths and combination packages.
- Focused on presenting treatment durations and prices consistently.
- Made promotional offers easier to distinguish from standard treatments.

**Problem addressed:** Inconsistent treatment durations, promotional offers and pricing presentation could confuse customers comparing services.

### 3. Simplified Booking and Branch Selection
- Prioritised branch selection as an important step in the booking journey.
- Made WhatsApp and online booking actions clearer.
- Reduced competing contact pathways to create a more straightforward customer journey.

**Problem addressed:** Multiple booking prompts and contact options, including WhatsApp, Telegram and online booking, made it harder for customers to identify the next step.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage focuses on the following improvements:

- **Visual presentation:** A more distinctive luxury wellness aesthetic reflecting Armonia's massage and Moroccan bath experiences.
- **Layout:** Clearer separation of treatment categories, packages, promotional offers and branch information.
- **User experience:** Easier treatment comparison and more intuitive navigation.
- **Consistency:** More structured presentation of treatment durations, prices and promotional information.
- **Mobile experience:** Clearer booking actions and easier access to branch-specific contact options.

## 5. Project Summary

The Armonia Spa homepage redesign addresses three key areas: brand differentiation, treatment and pricing clarity, and booking convenience.

The overall objective is to create a more polished luxury spa experience online, help customers understand available treatments, compare their options and move more easily from selecting a branch to making an appointment.
